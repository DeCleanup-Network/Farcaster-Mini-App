# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** May 2026

## 1. Introduction

DeCleanup Rewards ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share information when you use the DeCleanup Rewards application and services (collectively, the "Services"), including our Farcaster Mini App at [miniapp.decleanup.net](https://miniapp.decleanup.net) and related web properties.

By using our Services, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use our Services.

## 2. Information We Collect

### Information You Provide

- **Cleanup submissions:** Before and after photos, location data (geotag), and related metadata you submit when documenting cleanups
- **Wallet connection:** Your public wallet address when you connect a cryptocurrency wallet
- **Referral activity:** Referral relationships when you use or share referral links

### Information from Farcaster and Base Mini Apps

When you use the Services inside a Farcaster or Base Mini App client, we may receive:

- Farcaster ID (FID)
- Username, display name, and profile picture URL
- Mini App context provided by the host client

We do not receive your Farcaster or wallet private keys.

### Onchain and Public Blockchain Data

Because our Services operate on Base, certain information is recorded onchain and is publicly visible, including:

- Wallet addresses
- Cleanup submission records and verification status
- DCU points balances and reward claims
- Impact Product NFT ownership and levels
- Staking and verifier status
- Transaction hashes and timestamps

We do not control the permanence or public nature of onchain data.

### Automatically Collected Information

- **Device and browser data:** Browser type, operating system, and general usage data needed to operate the app
- **Local storage:** We store limited data in your browser (for example, pending cleanup IDs, onboarding progress, and verifier session flags) to improve your experience
- **Security and abuse prevention:** We may use bot detection and security services (such as Vercel Bot ID) to protect the Services

### Information We Do Not Collect

- Private keys or seed phrases
- Passwords (we do not operate traditional user accounts)
- Payment card or bank account information

## 3. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Provide, operate, and improve the Services
- Process and verify cleanup submissions
- Award DCU points, Impact Products, and token rewards
- Enable verifier workflows and community verification
- Display your profile, impact history, and onchain activity
- Send optional notifications (for example, Telegram alerts for new submissions to authorized channels)
- Prevent fraud, abuse, and automated bot activity
- Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service

## 4. How We Share Information

We may share information in the following circumstances:

### Public and Onchain

- Cleanup photos stored on IPFS are publicly accessible and generally cannot be deleted once published
- Onchain records on Base are public and permanent
- Your wallet address and associated onchain activity are visible to anyone

### Service Providers

We use third-party services to operate the Services, including:

- **Pinata** — IPFS storage for cleanup photos and metadata
- **Base / RPC providers** — blockchain connectivity
- **Neynar and similar APIs** — Farcaster user profile lookups
- **Vercel** — hosting and infrastructure
- **WalletConnect, RainbowKit, and wallet providers** — wallet connection
- **Telegram** — optional verifier or submission notifications (where configured)

These providers process data only as needed to perform their services.

### Legal Requirements

We may disclose information if required by law, regulation, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of users, the public, or our Services.

We do not sell your personal information.

## 5. IPFS and Photo Storage

When you submit cleanup photos:

- Photos are uploaded to IPFS via our server-side upload flow
- IPFS content is distributed and may be cached by public gateways
- Once stored on IPFS, photos cannot be reliably deleted or removed from all nodes
- Anyone with the content identifier (CID) or link may access the photos

Do not submit photos containing personal information you do not want to be public.

## 6. Location Data

If you enable location access when submitting a cleanup, we may collect geolocation coordinates associated with your submission. This data may be stored onchain and on IPFS as part of your cleanup record. You can decline location permissions, though some features may be limited.

## 7. Cookies and Local Storage

We use browser local storage for functional purposes such as:

- Tracking pending cleanup submission state
- Remembering onboarding completion
- Caching verifier session flags

We do not use local storage for third-party advertising. Third-party services integrated into the app (such as wallet providers) may set their own cookies or storage according to their policies.

## 8. Data Retention

- **Onchain data:** Retained indefinitely on the Base blockchain
- **IPFS content:** Retained indefinitely unless technically impractical to remove
- **Browser local storage:** Retained until you clear it or we invalidate it
- **Server logs:** Retained for a limited period for security and operations

## 9. Your Choices

You can:

- Choose not to connect a wallet (limited functionality)
- Decline camera or location permissions (limited functionality)
- Clear browser local storage in your device settings
- Stop using the Services at any time

Because much of our data is onchain or on IPFS, you may not be able to delete historical public records after submission.

## 10. Children's Privacy

Our Services are not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the Services, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

## 11. International Users

Our Services may be accessed globally. By using the Services, you understand that your information may be processed in countries where we or our service providers operate, which may have different data protection laws than your jurisdiction.

## 12. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed through our Services. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure. You are responsible for securing your wallet and private keys.

## 13. Third-Party Links and Services
